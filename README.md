📊 Online Shopping Sales Dashboard (Excel)

This project showcases an interactive sales dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze online retail performance from 2011 to 2015. It brings together multiple visualizations to provide a clear overview of business performance across categories, regions, products, and time. The dashboard highlights total sales by category, where Technology leads significantly, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies. A year-on-year trend indicates consistent growth, reflecting a steady expansion of the business. Profit distribution reveals that Technology contributes the majority share (51%), while Office Supplies account for a substantial portion and Furniture lags behind with minimal profitability, suggesting potential cost or pricing inefficiencies.

The dashboard also captures product-level insights through the top 10 highest-selling items, where products like Phones and Chairs dominate, indicating strong demand in both technology and home-related segments. Monthly order trends reveal fluctuations and seasonal patterns, which can be useful for planning promotions and inventory. Regional analysis shows that the West and East regions generate the highest sales, whereas the South underperforms, pointing to opportunities for targeted market expansion. Additionally, geographic visualization of top-performing states highlights that sales are concentrated in specific areas rather than evenly distributed.

Overall, the dashboard demonstrates how data visualization can be used to uncover actionable insights, such as focusing on high-margin categories like Technology, improving the profitability of underperforming segments like Furniture, and leveraging regional trends for strategic growth. Built using Excel features like pivot tables, charts, and map visualizations, this project serves as a practical example of transforming raw data into meaningful business intelligence.

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts  
- Slicers for interactivity  
- Map Visualizations  
- Data Cleaning & Aggregation  

## 📈 Key Insights
- Consistent year-over-year sales growth indicates business expansion  
- Technology is the most profitable category, contributing over half of total profit  
- Furniture shows low profitability despite reasonable sales, suggesting optimization opportunities  
- Sales are regionally concentrated, with growth potential in underperforming areas  
- Seasonal order patterns can inform marketing and inventory strategies  

## 📌 Conclusion
This dashboard provides a clear, data-driven view of sales performance and supports better decision-making by identifying key trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement.
